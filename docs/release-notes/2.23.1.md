Connecting Google Calendar no longer asks you to build anything first.

2.23.0 shipped Google Calendar support that required you to create your own Google Cloud project and OAuth client before you could sign in. That was the wrong trade. MeetingIntro now ships its own Google client, the same way it already ships one for Microsoft 365 — so connecting Google is a single **Sign in with Google**, and nothing else.

If your organization requires the app to run under its own Google Cloud project, the field is still there, folded away under the account panel. Put something unusable in it and the app quietly falls back to the built-in one and says so, rather than leaving you with a sign-in that fails for reasons nothing explains.
