### New

**The dropdown can now fit itself to your day.** Turn on **Fit dropdown height to content** in Settings → Menu Bar (rich popover style).

With one meeting today, the dropdown used to reserve a tall block for the list and leave a slab of empty space above the actions — plus a scrollbar for a list that had nothing to scroll. With this on, the list is exactly as tall as its rows: on a typical laptop screen a single meeting reclaims about 250pt of dead space.

It works the other way too. A busy day grows the dropdown to fit rather than squeezing everything into a fixed 300pt scroller — up to 70% of your screen height, after which it scrolls. Fourteen meetings get a 468pt list instead of a 300pt one.

Applies to Today, Upcoming, and Tasks alike, so switching tabs resizes the window to match. Off by default; nothing changes unless you turn it on.
