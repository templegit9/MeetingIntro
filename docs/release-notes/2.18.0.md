### New

**Camera cover reminder** — for anyone who slides a physical cover over their lens and then forgets it after a call.

Turn it on in **Settings → Plugins → Camera Cover** (it's off by default). Once you're out of meetings, you get a single notification with its own distinct sound: *"Close camera flap if open."*

- **It fires 2 minutes after a meeting ends** — not the instant it ends, while you're still saying goodbye. Configurable.
- **It also catches calls that were never on your calendar**, which is usually where a cover gets forgotten.
- **It stays quiet when it should:** while a meeting is still running, while your camera is genuinely still in use (a call that ran long), and when your next meeting is less than 10 minutes away — you weren't going to close it for a six-minute gap anyway.
- **It never repeats.** One nudge per event.

**MeetingIntro cannot see your cover, and doesn't pretend to.** No app can — and checking by quietly taking a photo would require camera permission *and* light the green camera LED every single time, which is a poor trade for a privacy feature. So the wording is deliberately conditional: it reminds you to close the flap *if* it's open; it never claims your camera is exposed.

Knowing whether the camera is in use costs nothing: it reads a system property and never opens the camera, so there's **no permission prompt, no green light, and no menu-bar dot**.
