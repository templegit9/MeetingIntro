Invitations on a work calendar that syncs into macOS Calendar can now be answered.

If your Microsoft 365 account reaches MeetingIntro through *both* macOS Calendar and the Microsoft 365 sign-in — which is the normal setup for a work Mac — every invitation was saying "this account can't send replies". The app keeps the macOS Calendar copy when a meeting arrives twice, and that copy is the one Apple gives no way to RSVP with. So the feature did nothing at all for precisely the people most likely to want it.

It now remembers the Microsoft 365 identity of a meeting it saw twice, and sends your reply through that, while everything else — reminders you've dismissed, meetings you've armed for auto-join — stays keyed to the copy it has always used. Accept, Tentative, Decline and Propose all work on those invitations now.

One thing you may notice: your reply is sent immediately, but the meeting's status in the list comes from macOS Calendar and only updates once it syncs back from the server. The invitation drops out of the list as soon as you answer, so this only shows if you quit and reopen the app in that window — and what you'd see then is what your calendar genuinely still believes.
