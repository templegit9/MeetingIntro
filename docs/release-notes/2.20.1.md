### New

**Signing in to Microsoft 365 is now one click.**

Connecting a work calendar previously meant creating your own app registration in the Azure Portal, pasting a client ID, then copying a code into a Microsoft sign-in page by hand. That was a developer's setup process, not something to ask of anyone.

- **No Azure Portal.** MeetingIntro now ships its own app registration. There's still a field if you'd rather use your own, tucked away as an option.
- **No codes to copy.** **Settings → Calendar → Sign in with Microsoft** opens your browser, you approve, and you're back in the app.
- **Shared calendars included.** Sign-in now also asks for access to calendars shared with you.

**If your workplace blocks it, you now get a next step instead of an error.** Most organisations require an IT administrator to approve calendar access for any third-party app — that's Microsoft's default and nothing an app can bypass. Rather than showing a raw error code, MeetingIntro now says so plainly and gives you a link to send your administrator. Approving it once covers everyone at your organisation.
