### Fixes

**The camera cover reminder now respects Do Not Disturb.** It played its sound even while Focus was on — the banner was correctly held back by macOS, but the sound came from the app directly and slipped past. So you got a noise with nothing on screen to explain it, during the one mode where you'd asked not to be disturbed. The reminder now stays completely silent while Focus is on.

If you use the **Test the reminder** button with Focus on, Settings now tells you upfront that macOS will hold the banner — previously it looked like the feature was broken.
