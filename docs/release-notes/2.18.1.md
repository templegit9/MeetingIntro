### New

**The camera cover reminder now reaches you in Do Not Disturb** — and it varies what it says.

- **Do Not Disturb no longer swallows it.** macOS holds notification banners during Focus, so the reminder now appears as a small panel in the corner instead, which Focus doesn't govern. There's a toggle — *"Remind me even in Do Not Disturb"* — on by default; turn it off and the reminder stays completely silent during Focus, sound included.
- **The wording rotates** between six phrasings — *"Is your meeting over? · If so, close your camera cover."*, *"Camera check · Is the cover back on?"*, and others — so it doesn't turn into wallpaper you stop seeing. Each one asks rather than tells, because the app genuinely can't see your cover.
- **Testing under Focus now shows you what Focus will actually give you**, rather than playing a sound with no visible banner.

### Fixes

Previously the reminder's sound played even with Do Not Disturb on while the banner was held back — a noise with nothing on screen to explain it.
