An invitation you answered stays answered.

Accepting or declining sent the reply immediately, but the app then forgot it had done so. The meeting's status in the list comes from your calendar, which only catches up when it next syncs — so until then the invitation looked unanswered again, and after a restart, or once the meeting started, it came back asking you to accept something you had already accepted.

The reply is now remembered until your calendar agrees with it. The row confirming what you chose stays put for exactly that long — not on a timer, and no longer disappearing the moment the meeting begins.
