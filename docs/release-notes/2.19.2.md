### Fixes

**No more half-rows sliced against the menu.** The dropdown's meeting list could cut a row through the middle at its bottom edge, with the New Event row sitting right on the cut — it read as a rendering glitch rather than "there's more below".

- With **Fit dropdown height to content** on, the list now sizes to a whole number of rows, so the bottom edge always falls between rows.
- With it off, a soft fade at the bottom edge makes a partially visible row read as continuing rather than clipped.

**Calendar mirrors can recover after you change their sources.** If you edited a mirror to point at different calendars, the safety brake — which stops a sync that would delete an unusual number of copies — could trip and then refuse to let go: re-saving or clicking Re-enable cleared it, the next sync hit the same condition, and the mirror silently did nothing no matter how many times you tried.

Changing which calendars a mirror covers now counts as your intent, so the cleanup happens. The button is clearer too: **"Re-enable and sync"**, and the message tells you exactly how many copies will be removed before you press it. The brake still protects the case it was built for — a source calendar that briefly returns nothing — and a rename still doesn't authorise anything.

**Fewer wasted cycles, and a log you can trust.** A reminder's notification and voice path could re-run about ten times for the same meeting at the same threshold. You never saw ten notifications — those were caught downstream — but the app was redoing the work each time, and the diagnostic log claimed ten reminders where one had fired, which is misleading precisely when you are trying to work out why something did or didn't happen.

**Calendar changes no longer cause a refresh storm.** Every edit in Calendar.app, and every write a mirror makes, told the app to re-read your calendar immediately. On a busy day that meant dozens of extra full refreshes, each one re-running reminders and mirror syncing behind them. Those bursts are now collapsed into a single refresh, while granting calendar access still updates the app right away.

**The camera cover reminder waits instead of giving up.** If a check landed while a meeting was still running or the camera was still in use, the nudge was discarded. It now waits and tries again for about ten minutes, so a day of back-to-back meetings still gets its reminder.
