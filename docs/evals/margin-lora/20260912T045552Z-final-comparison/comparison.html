Assessment: improved, but not ready to ship

The pilot fixed all seven original failed cases in all three repetitions. It retained 86 of the control’s 89 fully passing cases and introduced three regressions, each failing 3/3.

  • Calendar move: omitted the destination date for “shift tomorrow’s 1:1 to Thursday”.
  • All-day batch (critical): included all three events and correct dates, but omitted all_day=true for each. Midnight is not an all-day event.
  • Research: gave the correct facts but omitted the required source citation.

Accuracy rose from 92.71% to 96.88%; median latency improved by 20.1% and p95 by 33.1%. The 97% accuracy gate and zero-critical-failure gate are not met. The three critical failures are three repetitions of one case, not three distinct critical cases.

Next: repair training coverage for these behaviors while retaining broad rehearsal examples; freeze fresh paraphrase and adversarial holdouts before another pilot. Keep this run and its grader unchanged. Training loss alone did not predict these regressions.
