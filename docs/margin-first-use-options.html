Margin / first-use design options

Before the notebook prepares itself.

Three popup treatments shown when someone first turns on Margin. These are interactive copy and layout mockups; no button downloads or changes anything.

A · Quick introduction Selected

Shortest read. Details stay expandable.

Meet Margin

A notebook that helps with your files, tasks and calendar.

About 14 GB
One-time download to prepare AI on this Mac.
  • No extra app or account to set up.
  • Your AI conversations run on this Mac.
  • You review changes before they happen.
What else should I know?

Setup time depends on your connection. You can cancel and retry later. Margin checks that your Mac has enough memory and storage before downloading.

Web research sends your search query to a search provider. Files and calendar actions need the access you grant. Memory learns from your messages; you can inspect, edit or turn it off.

Trade-off: the essentials are visible, but learning and web-search expectations need an extra click.

B · Clear expectations

One page, with the important details visible.

Your notebook, on your Mac

Margin can find files, help with tasks and prepare calendar changes.

Prepare once

About 14 GB downloads the first time. Margin handles setup and checks your Mac first. You can cancel and retry later.

Local by default

AI runs on your Mac. Web research sends your search query online; connected calendar actions use your calendar service.

You stay in control

Grant the folders and calendar access you want to use. Margin shows changes for you to confirm.

It learns how you like to work

Memory and voice evolve from your messages. You can inspect, edit or pause that learning in settings.

Best balance: honest about the download, privacy and permissions without making users understand the technology.

C · Guided setup

Three small steps. More guidance, more clicks.

Step 1 of 3

Trade-off: easiest to explain gradually, but the setup feels larger and users may click through without reading.

Same behavior behind every option

  • The popup appears before the first download. “Not now” leaves Margin off.
  • “Prepare Margin” starts compatibility checks and shows real download progress with cancel/retry controls.
  • Existing working installations skip the download. Routine MeetingIntro updates preserve the prepared model.
  • No Terminal commands, engine names, model picker or separate Ollama installation in this flow.
  • The download estimate must come from the shipped model manifest. The current default model is approximately 14 GB; hardware qualification and fresh-Mac setup verification are still implementation work.
