# How to Use MeetingIntro

MeetingIntro runs silently in your macOS menu bar (check the clock-checkmark icon at the top right of your screen) and ensures you never miss a meeting.

## 1. Connect Your Calendar
Go to the Calendar tab and choose either Apple EventKit (local Mac calendars) or Microsoft Graph (Office 365). Select which specific calendars to monitor for upcoming meetings.

## 2. Set Up Countdowns
In the Countdown tab, enable times (like 5 minutes before) and choose how you want to be alerted for each: via a floating Overlay window, a standard macOS System Notification, or a spoken Voice Announcement.

## 3. Choose Alert Sounds
In the Sounds tab, download and select a free Mixkit sound effect. This sound will play whenever a System Notification is triggered.

## 4. Pre-Meeting Lobby Music
In the Audio tab, pick a track to play automatically in the background while the countdown overlay is visible. This sets the tone before your meeting starts.

## 5. Customize Voice Prompts
In the Voice tab, you can customize the exact phrase your Mac speaks when a Voice Announcement is triggered. Use placeholders like `{{meeting}}` and `{{time}}`.
